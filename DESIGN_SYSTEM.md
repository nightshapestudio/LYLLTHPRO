# LYLLTH Visual System

## Direction

LYLLTH is the desktop instrument in the NIGHTSHAPE family. It inherits DrumKit's severe matte chassis, custom typography, geometric precision, sparse cool light, and selective signal color. It does not imitate a generic DAW, expand every iPhone control, or turn the three accent colors into decoration.

The arrangement is the persistent focal plane. Library, mixer, and inspector are tools that can leave the stage. More screen space is used for musical context and deeper editing, not for more chrome.

## Transferred directly from DrumKit

- `NIGHTSHAPE-Bold` for product branding
- Adam Light, Medium, and Bold for interface typography
- matte `#0C0C0E` chassis and slightly separated black panel tiers
- cool chrome-white primary type with readable gray secondary tiers
- cyan-teal, indigo, and purple as state and signal colors
- crisp one-pixel boundaries, square control geometry, steady LEDs, and restrained glow
- thin line-based meters, waveforms, envelopes, and automation displays
- compact hardware-like controls and persistent transport feedback

## Adapted for macOS

- pointer-sized targets, hover help, keyboard transport, text entry, and resizable layout
- a wide timeline in place of the iPhone's paged grid
- collapsible library, mixer, and inspector surrounding one dominant canvas
- track headers that use a small signal marker rather than full-height color stripes
- an inspector that presents a vertical signal chain and instrument controls
- denser information than the phone, but with stronger typographic hierarchy and more negative space

## Intentionally not transferred

- fixed iPhone geometry and edge gestures
- decorative landing-screen line art as permanent workstation wallpaper
- full-width mobile cards or stacked modal navigation
- simultaneous cyan/indigo/purple gradients
- tiny all-caps text as the default for all information

## Rules

1. One area leads. Tool panes must not compete with the current musical surface.
2. Accent color communicates source, selection, signal, or active state.
3. A panel is separated by value and one hairline, not a shadowed card.
4. Labels use Adam; the LYLLTH wordmark always uses `NIGHTSHAPE-Bold`.
5. Glow is limited to a live LED, transport state, or similarly meaningful event.
6. Prefer a waveform, envelope, meter, or useful musical preview over decorative graphics.
7. Panels must collapse without changing the document or audio state.
8. Narrow layouts may hide the inspector, but the arrangement and transport remain usable.
