# LUNATK factory bank

274 presets. Every one is rendered through the engine in a musical phrase and mixed against a reference arrangement, then gated on loudness, headroom, low end, top end, width, mono fold-down, tails, tuning, macro range, MOTION, velocity and CPU (`tools/lunatk_bank/build.py`).

## Macros (every preset)

- **MACRO 1 TONE**: darker below its default, brighter above.
- **MACRO 2 MOTION**: scales all movement; at 0 the sound stands still.
- **MACRO 3 SPACE**: drier below its default, wetter above.
- **MACRO 4 GRIT**: 0 is the designed tone; up adds drive and damage.
